It stands as a locally celebrated yet internationally renowned cultural tourism icon and one of Hong Kong’s most representative heritage brands. Today, Dukling operates as a sightseeing vessel cruising across Victoria Harbour, meticulously preserving precious artifacts from its seafaring past. On board, passengers can discover traditional fisherman cultural relics, including a statue of Tin Hau (Mazu)—the patron goddess of seafarers—and air-dried gourds traditionally used as life-saving tools for children. Seamlessly blending marine sightseeing with immersive cultural experiences, Dukling is consistently ranked among Hong Kong’s top ten tourist attractions. The Classic Victoria Harbour Cruise Experience This captivating 45-minute journey aboard Dukling offers an unparalleled perspective of the breathtaking Victoria Harbour. From the bustling energy of the daytime and the romance of the sunset to the dazzling brilliance of ‘A Symphony of Lights’—three distinct settings offer three unique charms, all waiting for you to experience firsthand. Don’t miss out on this essential harbour cruise crafted for every traveler. Book your tickets now!
