Passenger Reminder

HONG KONG

Visitors to Hong Kong must hold valid travel document.  Nationals of most countries are not required to obtain visas and can stay for periods varying from seven days to 180 days, depending on nationality. For more information about Visa requirements, please refer to the Immigration Department’s website: www.immd.gov.hk

* With effect from 23 January 2017, nationals of India will be required to successfully complete online pre-arrival registration before visiting Hong Kong visa-free.   For details, please visit the Immigration Department’s website.

MACAU

Macau grants visa free entry to over 50 countries or territories for a stay of 30 to 90 days. If a visa is required, it can be obtained upon arrival in Macau, issued by the Immigration Department for a period of 30 days.

* With effect from 1 Sept 2008, Mainland residents travelling on ‘Exit-Entry Permit for Travelling to Hong Kong and Macau’ without an exit endorsement for Macau will not be permitted to enter Macau from Hong Kong by the Macau Authorities.

** Starting from 1 July 2010, nationals of Bangladesh, Nepal, Nigeria, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Vietnam are required to obtain an entry visa before arriving at Macau.

For more details, please visit: http://www.fsm.gov.mo/psp/eng/EDoN.html

Dukling (Victoria Harbour Cruise)
Sailing Time – Approx. 45 mins

About Dukling
Built in 1955, Dukling is the most historically significant and currently the only regularly scheduled three-masted Chinese junk boat sailing in Hong Kong. It stands as a locally celebrated yet internationally renowned cultural tourism icon and one of Hong Kong’s most representative heritage brands. Today, Dukling operates as a sightseeing vessel cruising across Victoria Harbour, meticulously preserving precious artifacts from its seafaring past. On board, passengers can discover traditional fisherman cultural relics, including a statue of Tin Hau (Mazu)—the patron goddess of seafarers—and air-dried gourds traditionally used as life-saving tools for children. Seamlessly blending marine sightseeing with immersive cultural experiences, Dukling is consistently ranked among Hong Kong’s top ten tourist attractions.

The Classic Victoria Harbour Cruise Experience
This captivating 45-minute journey aboard Dukling offers an unparalleled perspective of the breathtaking Victoria Harbour. From the bustling energy of the daytime and the romance of the sunset to the dazzling brilliance of ‘A Symphony of Lights’—three distinct settings offer three unique charms, all waiting for you to experience firsthand. Don’t miss out on this essential harbour cruise crafted for every traveler. Book your tickets now!
